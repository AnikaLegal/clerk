Having Trouble Getting Your Bond Back?

If your landlord is holding onto your bond or you're not sure what's fair, we're here to help.

We offer free, confidential and online legal help to renters across Victoria and help you understand what to do next.

Does this sound like you?

  • Your bond is being held by the RTBA
  • You've moved out of your Property
  • Your landlord has applied to RDRV/ VCAT to try and claim some of your bond
  • You're not sure if the deductions are fair
  • You've been charged for things you don't agree with
  • You're not sure what your rights are

Understanding your options

If you're having trouble getting your bond back, it's a good idea to get in touch. Depending on your situation, you may be able to challenge deductions, negotiate with your landlord, or take steps to recover your bond.

We help you understand what's fair, explain your rights in plain language, and work through your options so you feel more confident about what to do next.

FAQ

How long should it take to get my bond back?
There are set processes for returning a bond, but delays can happen. If you're unsure what's normal in your situation, we help you understand what to expect.
Can my landlord keep my bond?
Your landlord can only claim part or all of your bond in certain situations. If something doesn't feel right, it's worth getting in touch so we can discuss your options.
What if I don't agree with the deductions?
You may be able to challenge the claim depending on your situation. We can help you understand what's fair and what steps you can take.
What if I haven't heard anything about my bond?
If you've moved out and haven't received your bond or any updates, there may be steps you can take to follow it up. Learn more about claiming your bond after your tenancy has ended.
Do I need evidence or documents to get help?
All we need to start your case is a copy of your landlord's application to RDRV or VCAT. You don't need to have anything else ready. We help you work through what you have in relation to your situation.

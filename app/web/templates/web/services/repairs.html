Understanding your options

If you're dealing with repairs that aren't being fixed, it's a good idea to seek advice. Depending on your situation, you may be able to follow up formally, arrange urgent repairs, or take further steps if your landlord isn't responding.

We help you understand what's considered urgent, explain your rights in plain language, and work through your options so you feel more confident about what to do next.

FAQ

What are urgent and non-urgent repairs?
Urgent repairs usually include issues that make your home unsafe or difficult to live in, like serious leaks, broken heating, or electrical problems. Non-urgent repairs are any other issues that require fixing so that you can comfortably use your home. If you're unsure, we help you understand your situation.
What if my landlord ignores my repair requests?
If your landlord isn't responding, there may be steps you can take to escalate the issue. We help you understand your options and what to do next.
Can I arrange repairs myself?
In some situations, you may be able to organise urgent repairs yourself, but there are rules around this. We help you understand what applies to you.
How quickly should repairs be fixed?
Urgent repairs should be addressed immediately. Non-urgent repairs must be fixed within 14 days. If there are delays, it's worth getting advice early.
Do I need proof of the issue?
No, that's okay. You don't need to have everything ready right now. We help you work through what you have in relation to your situation.

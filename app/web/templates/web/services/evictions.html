If you're facing eviction, it's a good idea to get legal advice early. Depending on your situation, you may be able to stay in your home longer, challenge the notice, negotiate with your landlord, or get more time to move.

FAQ

Can I be evicted straight away?
There's a strict legal process your landlord needs to follow. Your landlord must give you the minimum notice period required by the law. If you don't move out by the time your notice expires, the landlord must apply to VCAT to evict you. You can't be forced to leave the Property without a VCAT order and a warrant for possession from Police.
What is a notice to vacate?
It's a formal document from your landlord asking you to leave. You can only be given a notice for particular reasons under the law, and the notice itself needs to meet certain requirements to be valid.
What if I don't think it's fair?
You might be able to challenge it, depending on your situation. We help you understand your options.
How quickly should I act?
It's best to get legal advice before taking action. The sooner you get support, the more options you may have.
What if I don't understand the notice I received?
That's okay - you don't need to figure it out on your own. We help you make sense of it.

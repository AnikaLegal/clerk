How It Works

Getting legal help can feel overwhelming, especially when you're already dealing with stress around your housing.

We keep the process simple, clear, and supportive so you understand what happens next and how we can help.

{# INTRODUCTION #}

Getting help is simpler than you think

You don't need to know exactly what your rights are before reaching out, and you don't need to have everything figured out.

Whether you're dealing with an eviction, rental repairs or bond issues, our team of volunteer paralegals and lawyers help you understand your options and the steps you can take next.

We offer free, confidential, online legal help to renters across Victoria.

{# URGENT CTA — Facing eviction #}

Facing eviction? We can help

Some rental issues are more pressing than others. If you're facing eviction, it's a good idea to get legal advice early. Depending on your situation, you may be able to stay in your home longer, challenge the notice, negotiate with your landlord, or get more time to move.

We help you understand your notice, explain your rights in plain language, and work through your options so you feel more confident about what to do next.

{# WHAT HAPPENS NEXT — Steps #}

What happens after you contact us?

1
Tell us what's going on

Start by completing our online intake form. We'll ask a few questions about your rental situation so we can understand how to help.

The form takes around 10 minutes to complete.

You don't need to have every document or detail ready before getting started.

2
We review your situation

Once we receive your form, our team reviews your information.

We look at the type of rental issue you're experiencing, whether we're the right service to help, and how urgent your situation may be.

If we need more information, we may contact you to better understand your situation.

3
We contact you about next steps

If we're able to assist, one of our trained volunteer paralegals, supervised by a lawyer, will get in touch to explain your options and discuss what support may be available.

Depending on your situation, we can help you to understand your rights, review notices or documents, communicate with your landlord or agent, and understand available pathways and support options.

Every situation is different, and we work through each matter individually.

{# FAQ #}

FAQ

How long does it take to hear back?
Response times can vary depending on demand and the urgency of your situation.
We usually acknowledge new requests within 2 business days. Sometimes matters are assigned quickly, while at other times there may be a wait depending on demand and the urgency of the situation.
Do I need any documents before contacting you?
No, that's okay. You don't need to have everything ready before reaching out. We can help you work through what you have.
Is the service really free?
Yes. We provide free legal help to eligible renters across Victoria. Check your eligibility here.
Will I speak with a real person?
Yes. Every request is reviewed individually by our team. If we're able to assist, your matter will be managed by one of our trained volunteer paralegals, supervised by a lawyer, who will work with you to understand your situation and explain your options.
What if I'm not sure whether my issue qualifies?
That's okay. If something doesn't feel right with your rental situation, it's still worth getting in touch.
Will you represent me at VCAT?
We help renters understand tribunal processes and prepare for mediation or hearings, including matters that progress to RDRV or VCAT. We support renters to represent themselves at VCAT.
